## NEW findings

### G1-L-1 — LOW — Reprocessed webhooks (E4-M-2 crash-retry, F2-L-1 concurrent duplicate) send duplicate customer lifecycle emails — the F1-L-2/F2-L-1 fixes guarded money, not notifications

**Files:** `backend/src/providers/stripe.ts:781, 915, 932, 935, 973` (`emitBillingEvent` call sites); `backend/src/services/billing.ts:68-107` (`registerDefaultBillingHooks`, registered in `index.ts:188` and `worker.ts:133`).

**What's wrong:** The pass#6 fixes made reprocessing safe for *money* effects (coupon redeem guarded by `coupon_redemptions.event_id`; refund credits guarded by `tenant_credits.webhook_event_id`) — and the fix comment in `stripe.ts:469-473` claims *"Reprocessing is safe because every non-idempotent handler below is guarded by an event-id idempotency key"*. That claim is false for the **notification** side effects. Every handler branch ends with an unconditional `emitBillingEvent(...)`, and the default hook sends real customer emails for `invoice.payment_failed`, `subscription.past_due`, `subscription.downgraded`, and `charge.refunded` with **no event-id dedupe** (the `notification_log` insert is write-only; nothing checks it).

**Concrete trigger:**
1. Stripe delivers `charge.refunded` (or `invoice.payment_failed`, or a grace-branch `customer.subscription.updated(past_due)`).
2. Attempt 1 runs the handler — "Refund processed" / "Payment failed — please update your payment method" email goes out — then crashes before `markProcessed()` (E4-M-2's exact scenario), or a concurrent duplicate delivery loses the ledger insert race (F2-L-1's scenario).
3. The retry sees `processed_at IS NULL` and reprocesses the full handler → the customer gets the **same lifecycle email twice**.

Duplicate dunning/refund emails are customer-visible and erode trust; `charge.refunded` double-emails are the worst case (money moved once, customer told twice). The money guards are all correct — only the notification path leaks.

**Why LOW:** bounded (one extra email per crash-retry/concurrency incident — a rare window), no money movement, no data loss, no privilege issue.

**Fix:** give the email hook the same event-id idempotency as the money paths — e.g. thread the webhook `eventId` into `emitBillingEvent` and have the default hook record `notification_log(event_id)`-style dedupe (`INSERT ... ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` before sending), or hoist `markProcessed()`-style "email emitted" tracking into the `stripe_webhook_events` ledger flow so a reprocessed event skips notifications while still applying money/idempotent state.

**Duplicate check:** grepped all `.audit-pass{1,2,3,4,5,6}-*.md` for `emitBillingEvent`, `registerDefaultBillingHooks`, `notification_log`, duplicate/dunning/lifecycle emails. Prior passes filed state-resurrection-wrongly-sent-dunning (D4-D5, E4-M-3) — different defects (newer event, wrong state). No pass discusses duplicate notifications from reprocessing the *same* event id. **NEW.**
